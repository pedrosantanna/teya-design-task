# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Accent wash behind the name
The shop page SHALL show the shop's accent wash behind its name and status lines, inset with the card at rest and edge to edge once the card fills the screen.

#### Scenario: Florist
- **WHEN** the shopper opens Halo Florist
- **THEN** its name sits on a Pistachio wash, in Display title
