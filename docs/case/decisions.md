# Key decisions

Six bets that shaped the product, each with what I gave up for it.

<!-- screenshot: the decision log open on decision 017 or 037, to show how a decision is recorded -->

## An agent that asks shops

The main way in is a concierge the shopper talks to (decisions 017 and 018). Browsing shops stays as the second way in. I gave up a catalogue-style search, which needs item data most independents don't have. The cost is time: an asked shop takes minutes to answer. If shoppers won't wait, browsing becomes the main screen.

## The request as the unit

Four objects, and no item pages (decisions 025 and 039). When items need different shops or days, Snuffle proposes a split and the shopper decides. I gave up one chat per product and one chat per shop, which would hand the chasing back to the shopper. The cost is a word, "request", that shoppers don't use.

## Buy, then collect

The action is Buy (decision 037). An asked shop's payment is only authorised, and taken when the shop confirms it has set the item aside. Each shop sets how long it keeps it, 7 days by default, with a full refund if nobody collects. I dropped Hold, which reads as a free reservation, and an unpaid reserve, where the shop carries the no-show risk (decision 032). Shoppers who pay cash are a blind spot.

## Shops without an app

The shop side has no interface (decisions 021 and 031). Shops answer on WhatsApp with numbered replies, because they already use it and need nothing installed. Numbered replies are rigid for the shop, and WhatsApp's 24-hour rule limits follow-ups to approved templates.

## The shopper's area

Snuffle looks from the shopper's location and deadline, across every high street within reach (decision 024). From Blythe Road six shopping streets are within a 15-minute walk; tying the shopper to one would hide five.

## Shops before interviews

I used one method on shoppers, public posts, and chose a proof of concept with about ten shops over interviews (decisions 015 and 020). What a shop does with a real request tells me more than what it says. The cost is building before I know whether shops answer.
