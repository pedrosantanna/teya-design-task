How the greys are built. shadcn/ui fills a set of semantic variables (background, muted, border, input, ring) from a base grey such as Tailwind stone, written in OKLCH. Radix gives each of 12 steps a job and offers tinted greys to sit next to an accent; Material 3 keeps the source hue at low chroma for its neutrals.
