# Tab placement

Where the switch between Nearby and Requests could live, now that the bottom of the screen belongs to the composer. Six options drawn on the same home, each weighed on the same information-architecture criteria and against how other apps solve it.

```summary
Takeaways:
- Six places: icon tabs under the header (v2), a segmented control, a switch just above the composer, a bottom bar with the ask bar as a pill in the middle, an inbox button in the header, and a side drawer.
- An agent's recommendation from the references: D, the bottom bar with Nearby and Requests at the sides of the ask pill. It is the only option high on finding Requests, thumb reach and platform conventions together, and it has room for a third mode. The fallback, if the large always-open ask bar must stay, is B with a count on the Requests segment.
- The options trade visibility of Requests against the space the composer needs at the bottom.
Call:
- Still to decide, after comparing them side by side. My criteria: whether a shopper finds Requests, one-thumb reach, competition with the composer, room for a third mode, and platform conventions on iOS and Android.
AI:
- An agent collected placement references on Mobbin. The main agent drew the six options on the v2 home and wrote the comparison.
Trade-offs:
- D, the most familiar pattern, shrinks the large ask bar into a pill that opens a full input screen, so asking looks less like the main way in.
```
