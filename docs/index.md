# Introduction

These are the working docs for a design take-home: an app that puts a high street's independent shops in one place, so a shopper can see what is available nearby right now, buy it, and collect it or have it delivered.

## Purpose

I use these docs to log the work I do with AI agents: what was researched, what I decided and why, what the agents produced, and what I kept or threw away. They're written for me, for the agents and for anyone else who joins the work.

The output for review is a recorded walkthrough, with this record as its evidence. So the pages aim to be complete and traceable, and the walkthrough tells the story.

## Phases

The docs follow the order of the work. Each phase starts once the one before it has produced something to build on.

| Phase | Question it answers | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | What is a high street today, who shops there, and what do they say about it? | Done |
| Define | Findability or transaction, for which shopper, under which assumptions? | In progress |
| Develop | What does the journey look like, and which concept holds up? | Not started |
| Deliver | The prototype, the polished screens and, if it's worth building, a running app | Not started |

The [decision log](decision-log.md) records the calls that shaped the process, in the order I made them.

## Evidence

Claims about people come from public posts, quoted word for word with the author's public handle, the network, the date and a link to the original. Everything is from the 18 months before November 2026. Where a page draws a conclusion from that material, it says so. [Sources](discover/sources.md) lists every link in one place.

## Use of AI

I directed the work and made the decisions. AI agents did most of the collecting, tagging and drafting: a main agent with subagents, and a second agent for reviews. Every page says how AI was used in that step, and [how AI was used](process/ai-log.md) logs what each agent was asked, what came back and what I threw away or fixed. The prototype is built in code. Figma and Apple Freeform are used only for exploration.
